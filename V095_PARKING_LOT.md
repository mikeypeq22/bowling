# v0.95+ ideas parked for future iterations

These ideas are **not yet implemented**.

1. **Personalized snack area:** buy a dedicated snack-counter TV, condiment stand, decorative menu board, extra stools/chairs, better fryer/oven. Display every purchased fixture in the room image. Some affect capacity or guest happiness; others purely cosmetic. Balance construction time vs instant delivery.
2. **Personalized bar:** select furniture, bartop, glass racks, improved refrigeration, illuminated signs, TVs, specialty drink stations; add staff requirements as needed.
3. **Pro shop catalog:** balls by purpose/quality, bags, shoes, cleaners, tape and grips, surface restoration, drilling equipment, fitting, services and dedicated stock shortages.
4. **Arcade expansion:** purchasable crane games, cabinets, prizes and consumables; maintenance and occasional machine downtime as alternatives to always-on revenue.
5. **Property progression:** second floor, two-story builds, additional rooms after floor grid fills, second locations with different specialties.
6. **First-time owner experience:** optional business-name customization, floor paint and distinct lobby designs during startup; possible one-click `Quick Start` for returning testers.
7. **Accessibility / mobile:** rearrange floor rooms by drag-and-drop; larger tap targets; alternate portrait shop previews.
8. **QA/infra:** real Google + email login and cloud saves tested on iPhone Safari; auth retry statistics without collecting personal data.
